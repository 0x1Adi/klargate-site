The alarm did not ring

  • Open EvilSnooze. If the list shows a disarmed banner, alarms are switched off: allow them in the iPhone Settings app under EvilSnooze.
  • Alarms play at the Ringtone and Alerts volume (Settings › Sounds & Haptics). Turn it most of the way up.
  • In EvilSnooze, open Settings › Support › Prepare diagnostics and send the file with the contact form. It contains alarm events and times only.

A task will not let me through

  • Walking and push-ups always offer Can't right now? Type instead.
  • Without Motion, the microphone or the camera, those tasks turn into typing. They never disappear.
  • Push-ups only appear if you switch them on in Settings › Random tasks.
  • Every morning ends by itself 90 minutes after the first ring.

Purchases

  • Restore EvilSnooze Plus from Settings › Purchases › Restore purchases, on the same Apple Account.
  • Cancel or change a subscription in the iPhone Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, or from EvilSnooze Settings › Manage subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
