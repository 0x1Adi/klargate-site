An alarm with a memory, and opinions. For iPhone.

Snooze once and it comes back harsher. Snooze again and you prove you are awake: a quick sum, a sentence to type, a memory grid, a shake, a selfie. Keep going and you get out of bed before it lets you off. Every morning ends with a receipt you can share, and one dry line from Mal, the alarm that resents being snoozed.
