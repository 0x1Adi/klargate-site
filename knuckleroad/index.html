Knuckle Road

One thumb. Zero manners. For iPhone.

Ride an endless 3D highway with one thumb. Drag to steer, flick to punch rivals off their bikes, and stay ahead of the patrol car that never, ever gives up. One Daily Highway for everyone, challenge links that let a friend race your ghost bike, and a Crash Cam clip of every wipeout. Free to play, no ads, no account.
