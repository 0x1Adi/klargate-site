Privacy policy

Knuckle Road for iPhone. Last updated 4 October 2026. Developer: Aditya Tiwari.

Knuckle Road has no accounts and no server of ours. Your progress, cash, unlocks, personal bests, streak and settings are stored only on your iPhone, and are deleted when you delete the app.

What is collected

Knuckle Road collects no data. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected", matching the privacy manifest shipped in the app. There is no analytics SDK and nothing is sent to us. The manifest also declares the standard required-reason APIs the app uses (user defaults, system boot time, file timestamps); none of that information leaves your iPhone.

Game Center (Apple)

Game Center is optional. If you sign in, your scores and achievements go to Apple, which processes them under Apple's privacy policy. Every other part of the game works without Game Center.

What a shared link or clip carries

Sharing is always your choice, through the system share sheet.

  • A challenge link (Bust Sheet › Challenge) carries only the run you chose to share: the seed, mode, highway, bike, distance, knockdowns and outcome, your rider name (at most 16 bytes, filtered), up to 9 crash spots, and a position trace of the run. The payload sits in the link's fragment (the part after #), which browsers never send to a server. The receiver's app decodes it on their device and drops it when the run ends.
  • A Crash Cam clip (Bust Sheet › Share clip) is a short video of your wipeout with the officer's roast line burned in; your rider name and plate can be visible in the footage. It goes only where you choose to share or save it.

Purchases (Apple)

Open Road is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details; we only learn on your device whether the purchase is active. Apple processes purchase data under its own privacy policy, linked above.

Notifications

One local reminder per day when the Daily Highway is still waiting. The app asks for notification permission only after your second Daily Highway run; the reminder is scheduled on your iPhone and nothing about it is sent anywhere. Turn it off in the app's Settings › Daily reminder, or in iPhone Settings › Knuckle Road › Notifications.

Photo library (add only)

Write-only access, requested by iOS only if you choose "Save Video" in the share sheet, so a Crash Cam clip can land in Photos. The app never reads your photo library. Sharing works without this permission.

This website

The pages at klargate.com (about, support, privacy and the challenge fallback page) are static files served by GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps standard web server access logs (IP address, browser, time) for every visit; see GitHub's privacy statement. The challenge payload is in the URL fragment, which is never sent to the server, so it cannot appear in those logs.

Retention and deletion

Everything the app stores is on your iPhone and is deleted when you delete the app. Game Center data is retained by Apple under its own policy. Messages you send through the contact form are kept only as long as needed to answer them.

Revoking consent and deletion requests

Turn off notifications and photo access in iPhone Settings › Knuckle Road, sign out of Game Center in iPhone Settings, and delete the app to remove everything it stored. For anything else, use the contact form below.

Tracking

Knuckle Road does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Knuckle Road is not directed at children and collects nothing that identifies anyone.
