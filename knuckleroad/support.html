A challenge link does not open the game

  • The link opens a page at klargate.com/knuckleroad/c/ with an Open in Knuckle Road button. Tap it with the game installed and the challenge run starts.
  • If the page says "This challenge link is broken", the link was cut off in transit. Ask your friend to send it again from the Bust Sheet's Challenge button.
  • If tapping the button does nothing, update Knuckle Road to the latest version from the App Store.

The Daily Highway will not let me ride

  • The Daily is one scored attempt per UTC day, for everyone. If the app closes mid-run, that attempt stays used.
  • The Daily card on Home counts down to the next one.
  • Open Road adds unlimited Daily practice runs that are never scored.

Save Video does nothing

  • Saving a Crash Cam clip needs Photos add access. Turn it on in the iPhone Settings app › Knuckle Road › Photos › Add Photos Only.
  • Sharing the clip through the share sheet works without this permission; only saving to Photos needs it.

The daily reminder never comes

  • The reminder is offered only after your second Daily Highway run, and it is one local notification per day at most.
  • Turn it on in the app under Home › Settings › Daily reminder, or in the iPhone Settings app › Knuckle Road › Notifications.

Purchases

  • Open Road is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel.
  • Restore it in the app under Home › Settings › Restore Purchases (also on the Open Road paywall), on the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sending a bug report

  • Home › Settings › Export diagnostics writes a short redacted text file you can paste into your message. It contains no other players' names and no challenge link payloads.

FAQ

How does the Daily Highway work?
Everyone gets the same road and traffic for the UTC day. You get one scored attempt. If the app closes mid-run, that attempt stays used. Open Road adds practice runs that are never scored.

What is a streak freeze?
Every 5 Dailies you play earns a freeze, up to 2. A freeze covers a missed day automatically. Freezes are earned only, never sold.

Does Free Ride get easier?
Yes, a little. After 3 Free Ride runs in a row under 400 m, traffic thins out by 25 %. A run over 800 m turns it off. It never applies to the Daily or to leaderboards.

How do helmets drop?
Each knockdown has a 5 % chance to drop a helmet. You get every helmet once before any repeats. Helmets are free and can't be bought.

Why does the unlock bar start partly full?
The first bike's bar starts with a head start because you found a wheel. The price doesn't change.

Did you use AI?
No image generators. Every model is built in code. Our tools were coded with Claude Code and our screens laid out with Claude Design.

What leaves my phone?
Scores and achievements go to Game Center if you're signed in. Challenge links carry your run and rider name only when you share them. Nothing else is sent.
